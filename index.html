Field Reference

Safety Co-Pilot Playbook

A working reference for using Microsoft Copilot on M365 in day-to-day safety work: inspections, workplans, regulation lookups and SOP drafting. Every prompt here is generic and reusable: copy it, swap in your own details and adjust the output to your unit's context.

Copilot drafts. It does not decide. Every output in this playbook still needs a human safety officer to check it against the current regulation and the actual site conditions before it's used.
