Section 06

Generation of Safety Standard Operating Procedures

These prompts draft a first-pass SOP from a task description. Every generated SOP still needs sign-off from the actual process owner and a check against current regulation before it's issued.

For the best results, upload or attach the relevant document (the existing SOP, task details or related regulation) before you prompt, so Copilot works from your actual source rather than general knowledge.

Prompt templates

Draft a new SOP from a task description
Act as a safety SOP writer. Context: the task is [describe the task, e.g. "refuelling a generator in the field"]. Known hazards include [list hazards]. Task: draft a Standard Operating Procedure covering scope, required PPE, step-by-step procedure, and emergency response. Format: use the headings Scope, PPE Required, Procedure (numbered steps), Emergency Response.
List the hazards you already know about. Copilot will then structure around them rather than guessing generic ones.
Update an SOP for a changed condition
Act as a safety SOP writer. Context: [paste the existing SOP]. The following has changed: [describe the change, e.g. "new equipment model" or "updated PPE standard"]. Task: revise the SOP to reflect this change, marking each section you modified. Format: keep the same heading structure as the original; add a change note at the top summarising what was updated and why.
Keeping the same structure makes it easy for reviewers to spot exactly what changed.
Convert an SOP into a quick-reference checklist
Act as a safety SOP writer. Context: [paste the full SOP]. Task: extract this into a one-page quick-reference checklist a person could follow on-site. Format: a checklist using short imperative steps, grouped under Before, During, After.
Useful for laminated field cards. Keep the full SOP as the authoritative document and this as the field-ready summary.
A generated SOP is a draft, not an approved procedure. It must go through your unit's normal review and sign-off process, and be checked against the current regulation, before it is issued or relied on.
Internal reference for the Safety Inspectorate. Prompts are starting templates; review outputs before use.
