Section 03

Query on Army Safety Regulations

Use these prompts to look up and interpret Army safety regulations, directives and policies. Always verify the cited reference yourself before relying on it.

For the best results, upload or attach the relevant document (the regulation, directive or policy in question) before you prompt, so Copilot works from your actual source rather than general knowledge.

Prompt templates

Find and explain a specific directive or policy (e.g. WBGT and Work Rest Cycles, 5M 4L, Safety Investigation Template, etc)
Act as a safety compliance assistant. Context: I have a query on [safety regulation name/type/definition]. Task: find the covering directive or policy on [topic, e.g. "working at height, work rest cycle, etc"] and explain it in plain language, including who it applies to and any exceptions. Format: a short paragraph, then a bullet list of key requirements, with the directive/regulation/policy cited for each point.
Good for looking up a specific directive such as WBGT and work rest cycles, 5M 4L or the Safety Investigation Template. Ask it to cite the directive or policy for each point so you can verify against the source.
Compare unit safety system against safety regulations
Act as a safety compliance analyst. Context: Compare my document uploaded [unit safety plan, safety briefing points, unit safety system, etc] against the Army Safety Requirements. Task: identify the gaps from my document focusing on requirements that affect [area, e.g. "PPE standards"]. Format: a [table, Word document, Excel sheet, etc] with my document content compared against the regulations and the associated gaps.
Upload your document first so Copilot can compare against it directly. Treat the gap list as a starting point and confirm each gap against the actual regulation.
Check a scenario against safety regulations
Act as a safety compliance assistant. Context: I have [regulation name] open. Task: assess whether the following scenario complies: [describe scenario in 2-3 sentences]. Identify any regulation, directive or policy that is or may be breached. Format: a verdict (compliant / non-compliant / unclear), followed by the reasoning and clause references.
Good for a quick first pass before escalating a genuinely ambiguous case to the actual regulation owner.
Copilot can misquote references or blend text from an outdated version if multiple documents are open. Cross-check every clause, directive or policy reference against the actual source before it appears in a report, workplan or SOP.
