Section 05

Generation of Safety Insights

These prompts turn raw incident logs, inspection notes or near-miss reports into patterns you can act on.

For the best results, upload or attach the relevant document (your incident log, inspection notes or near-miss data) before you prompt, so Copilot works from your actual source rather than general knowledge.

Prompt templates

Spot patterns in incident data
Act as a safety data analyst. Context: [paste or attach a list of incidents/near-misses with date, location, type, and cause]. Task: identify the top three recurring patterns by cause, location, or time of day. Format: a short paragraph per pattern, each followed by the supporting data points and one suggested corrective action.
The more structured your input data (consistent columns for date, type, cause), the more reliable the pattern-spotting will be.
Compare this period against the last
Act as a safety data analyst. Context: [paste this period's incident summary] and [paste last period's incident summary]. Task: compare the two and highlight what improved, what worsened, and anything new that appeared. Format: a table with columns Metric, This Period, Last Period, Change, Comment.
Good for the trend section of a recurring report. Pair it with the workplan briefing prompt in Section 04.
Turn inspection notes into a risk-ranked list
Act as a safety data analyst. Context: [paste raw inspection notes/observations]. Task: convert these into a risk-ranked list, ranking each observation by likelihood and severity. Format: a table with columns Observation, Likelihood (Low/Med/High), Severity (Low/Med/High), Overall Risk, Recommended Action.
Copilot's risk ranking is a starting judgement. Confirm high-severity items with your own assessment before publishing.
Copilot's patterns, comparisons and risk rankings are only as reliable as the data you give it, and it can still miscount or misjudge. Check every insight against the source data and your own assessment before it informs a decision or goes into a report.
Internal reference for the Army Safety Inspectorate. Prompts are starting templates; review outputs before use.
